## What the executable does

The Gummi menu draws its 2D side from three files:
- `gumimenu/<lang>/gumimenu.bar` `0000`, an ordinary 2LD ("gummi_menu_lay").
- The same BAR's `0004`, the layout transitions (FUN_140252e50, FUN_14027de60). Going from one screen to another plays the record's first scene, then its last once the first ends, and turns the 3D background.
- `gumimenu/menuentry.bar`, 632 item records (gm::MENUITEM, FUN_140270e80). Each names its layout, sequence, texture, six groups (appear, loop and close, selected and not) and the text drawn at the loop group's IS_ACTIVE anchor.

## Changes

`data::gummi_table`: `MenuEntry`, `read_menu_entries`, `Transition`, `read_transitions`. The 2LD itself already reads with `menu_layout::read_layd` and `read_imgz`.
