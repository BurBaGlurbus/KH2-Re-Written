## What the executable does (each confirmed against the reference)

1. **Curves past their ends** (FUN_1401d30c0): cycle type 1 continues along the last key's out-tangent or the first key's in-tangent. Type 2 repeats the key span.
2. **IK rotation channels:** a solved IK joint with flag 64 has its rotation channels rewritten from the solved matrix (FUN_1401ab290), and later expressions read them.
3. **Direction constraints** work in the parent's scaled frame, and position limits in unscaled local units (FUN_1401d3b80 inverts the scaled parent matrix).
4. **Expression curve nodes** (kinds 15, 43) sample curve `cdr` at `car × 60 / fps`; kind 43 returns degrees (FUN_1401d9950). The time node (kind 30) reads the motion clock, which the reference adapter leaves at 0.
5. **Scale constraint** (kind 6): the joint takes the source's world scale. A chain whose end joint lacks the chain-end flag is solved at its top joint.
6. **Chain target:** a chain reaches for its end joint's first position source only.
7. **Segment lengths:** a segment's length carries its joint's scale. Only a zero product is skipped. An aim flagged to use an empty effector list aims at the next joint.
8. **Chain ends:** a chain-solved end joint skips its position limit.

## Changes

## Uncertainty

- About 577 of the 594 left are float noise: rotation within 0.002 and translation within 2e-3 of the model's size. They come from giant rigs, rotation curves of thousands of radians, and nearly straight IK chains. That matches the PS2's approximate maths, which isn't emulated.
- **Unexplained:** B_HE020 202, where the aim target sits on the aiming joint.
- **Bad reference data:** B_HE020 203 and M_EX710 241 contain FLT_MAX or NaN.
- **Found but left out:**
  - The PC curve routine's rotation-step hold, because the PS2 reference doesn't have it.
  - Timed activation windows, which make the match worse. Deciding either needs PC captures.
- The expression clock difference is between the reference adapter and the game, not a known game fault.
