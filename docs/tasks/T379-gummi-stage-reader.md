## What the executable does

A flight loads `gumibattle/gm<route><level>.gbx` (FUN_1401f6f70; the letter is "abc"[level − 1], DAT_1405b4ca1). Its header holds:
- "GBX_", then u32 0x3c;
- the rail section and the script section, each as {u32 offset, u32 bytes};
- at +0x40, the name of the stage map (e.g. `gumibattle/gm00.map`), which FUN_1401e7d10 loads.

The rail (FUN_1401e8070) is 28-byte points: position, rotation in radians, and the cumulative distance. The script section (FUN_1401efb50) begins with seven {u32 offset, u32 count} tables.
