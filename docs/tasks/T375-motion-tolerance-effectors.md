## What changed

- **Pass rule** (`motion_eval_compare`, written up in its module doc as the owner's decision). A bone matches at a sample when:
  - its translation error is at most max(0.1, 2e-3 × the model's size at that sample);
  - each rotation row is within 0.002 of its length (0.002 absolute for rows shorter than 1).

  Coordinates of 1e6 or more don't count towards the size, and non-finite values never match. `STRICT=1` restores the plain 0.1 rule. The tool ends with a PASS/FAIL line.
- **External effector lists** (`data::motion`): FUN_1401d3b80 reads the list at header +0xa0 with no bound. The reader stopped at the extra block's start, so a motion with no extra block lost its list. The list now runs to the motion's end in that case, and a reader test on generated bytes covers it.
- T372's "an empty list aims at the next joint" fallback is removed. It matched M_EX730 5 only because that list's real first entry is the next joint.
