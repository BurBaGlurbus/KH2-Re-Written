## Steps and implementation

1. `world_map::events`: the event scripts of `libretto-wm.bar`
   (FUN_1403ec4d0's "libretto-%s.bar", table FUN_1403ec420) read into the
   commands of FUN_1403ec260 (wait, stop, fades) and FUN_14038f270 (message,
   preload, BGM, object widgets, event camera, refresh, layout, jumps, the
   Hollow Bastion effect), and `EventPlayer`, which runs them a step per
   tick: messages hold the script, op 0x104 waits its frames, op 0x105 is
   the pre-refresh, 240 frames, the refresh with sound 16008, 90 frames.
2. `world_map::camera::EventCamera` (FUN_140394150 / FUN_140394460): the
   eye on a cubic Bézier with inner points at 0.2 and 0.95, the view
   direction turning to −offset.
3. `visual::dialogue::TalkWindow::balloon`: YS::WINDOW_BALLOON as the
   message widget places it (FUN_140393760).
4. `kh2fm wm`: the queued events play at arrival one after another
   (FUN_14038dc60), holding the cursor and the reactions; the objects show
   the world state from before the events' scripts until an event
   refreshes them; message windows advance on decide; fades draw on top;
   the map's draw groups follow changes; the field BGM (the area's arif
   pair) plays; menu sounds (se000 1..5), the window's page sound and the
   refresh sound (se016) play through the mixer. `--event <id>` plays an
   event after the queued ones.
