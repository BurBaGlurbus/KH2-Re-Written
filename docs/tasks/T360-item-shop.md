## What the executable does

The spec holds it in full. In short: a shopkeeper's or SHOP_POINT's reaction shape runs a command with execute 0x7f, whose signal is the shop's id and goes no further than the shop. `shop` in 03system lists 21 shops and their goods. A town shop also sells the same categories from each Moogle shop visited. Buy and Sell are one list each, with quantity and Yes or No after them. Buy and sell prices are stored, not derived. There's no refusal message, only the error sound.

## Uncertainty

- The list's move and scroll are inferred from the wrap rule (FUN_1402a2380): the rows move, then the first row scrolls circularly.
- A new good's badge sits over its "Munny", which is what the group data gives (x -39 to +20 from the price's right end). This looks odd, and is worth checking first against the installed game.
- SHOP_POINT's class isn't traced past its constructor. The field shows its sub-object, the Moogle 0x487, at the placement, looping motion 0 [I]. The SHOP_POINT's own model (a Moogle with a balloon) drew largely black here for a reason not found, so it isn't used, and no balloon shows.
- Synthesis isn't built. Its row refuses with the error sound. Workshops have no goods until materials unlock, and materials' flags are only set by synthesis.
- FUN_1403c3fe0's side effects on a purchase (flag-type items, the new-consumable queue, magic and ability kinds) aren't run: a purchase only adds to the stock.
- Whether a spawn is story-gated isn't read beyond which event program registers it, and in normal play the room's event program may not register the shop's set.
- The model's turn and drop are read as degrees and screen units; their look was judged by eye. The panel's timing is not compared. `DAT_140abac59`'s forced close isn't built.
