## What the executable does

54 mission records of 0x40 bytes sit at save +0x100e0: nine routes of three levels, then the same 27 for EX (FUN_1402c76f0). The result screen writes them (FUN_140209750): best score u32 +0x00, ships destroyed +0x06, best medal value +0x1a, rank +0x1b, cleared +0x1d. The unlock rules (FUN_140209b50) set +0x1e, and the treasure share (0-100) is the float at +0x20. Earlier notes and `gummi.rs` called +0x1e "cleared" and +0x20 "score". The world map's route panel (FUN_140398ef0) reads +0x1e for its state and its EX mark, and +0x20 for its three digits.

## Changes
